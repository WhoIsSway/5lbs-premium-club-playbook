01 / Thesis

Training is the core. Premium is the system around it.

Working hypothesis: +5LBS should not become a generic “luxury gym.” The stronger opportunity is to extend professional training into the full before / during / after-training experience — assessment, coaching, recovery, nutrition, hospitality, community and member identity.

02 / Anchor benchmarks

Three different answers to “premium”.

Equinox / High-Performance Living

Performance as lifestyle

Training, recovery and community converge inside one membership ecosystem. Spa, Pilates, assessment and curated retail extend the performance promise beyond the gym floor.

Study: service integration, hospitality, member identity.
Risk: copying luxury finishes without a performance system.
Third Space / Luxury Health Club

Depth under one roof

Elite coaching is surrounded by recovery, sauna/steam, food, sports medicine, nutrition and member events. Recovery is also productized as a paid membership add-on.

Study: expert services + monetizable recovery.
Risk: too much service breadth before utilization is proven.
Life Time / Athletic Country Club

Own more of the day

Large resort-like clubs combine fitness with pools, racquet sports, food, spa, kids and work/living adjacencies. The model expands from workout time toward a broader healthy-life ecosystem.

Study: dwell time, family, sport and social ecosystem.
Risk: destination-club economics may not fit an urban +5LBS site.
03 / Investment map

Where can money create value?

Core / should invest

Training capability

Coaches & education · strength/cardio equipment · assessment · programming · member performance data · changing-room fundamentals.

Differentiator

Performance ecosystem

Recovery · sports rehab interfaces · nutrition · sleep/recovery guidance · concierge · premium training rituals · community competition.

Partner, not build

Specialist expertise

Sports medicine · diagnostics · advanced longevity services · selected F&B · clinical or high-complexity recovery where partners can outperform self-operation.

Low priority / prove first

Expensive theatre

Oversized social areas · low-use wellness tech · broad family facilities · destination-scale pools/racquets unless site, member demand and operating model clearly support them.

05 / Global radar

Adjacent models worth tracking.

HEIMAT / Los Angeles

Luxury fitness + living well

A 75,000 sq ft members-only club combining serious training and purpose-built boutique studios with a broader luxury club proposition.

Question for +5LBS: how much “club life” should surround training?
Remedy Place / US

Social wellness

Positions recovery and self-care as a social behavior rather than an isolated treatment: ice baths, sauna, hyperbaric oxygen, red light, functional medicine and social/group experiences.

Question for +5LBS: can recovery become community, not just equipment?
Next radar

Asia + performance clubs

Next research wave will prioritize high-performance clubs, longevity/recovery concepts and premium urban fitness in Japan, Korea, Singapore, Hong Kong and major European cities.

Filter: novel operating logic, not merely expensive design.
